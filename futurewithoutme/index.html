What This Is

The victors of the egress session have seemingly made a postludic world for themselves and their peoples back home: Ksetra, a world where the Game is the most important thing that ever happened and the Victors the most important people. It’s actually a play of shadows and mirrors between the godlike players Lord Spec, who’d make it a dollhouse, and Ladija, who’d make it a haunted house. Some of the Victors have been realizing that something is going deeply wrong. Are they right about what it is? — KB

This is a record of a kind of tennis game that KB and I are playing — we’re taking turns playing the Victors in the aftermath of the death (or is it an escape?) of fellow victor Aþollo.
